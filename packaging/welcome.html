HP LaserJet M1005: Print & Scan

This installer adds the native print driver and the HP M1005 Scan app.

Before continuing: connect the printer by USB and turn it on. The installer will configure printing when the M1005 is detected.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later. No Rust, Homebrew, Ghostscript, Rosetta or archived HP drivers are needed.

This is an independent open-source project. It is not an official HP driver.
