Ready to print and scan

Print: press Command-P in any app and choose HP LaserJet M1005 (Rust).

Scan: open HP M1005 Scan in Applications or Spotlight. Place a page face-down on the glass, choose PNG or PDF, and click Scan.

If the printer was disconnected during installation, connect and power it on, then open System Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add Printer. Select the M1005 and choose HP LaserJet M1005 - Native Rust under Use > Select Software.

For updates and help, visit github.com/ashishjangde/HP_M1005_Rust.
